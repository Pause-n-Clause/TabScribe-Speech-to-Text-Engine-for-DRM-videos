What is Tabscribe?

A local Python app that captures your PC's speaker output and transcribes it using OpenAI Whisper — offline, no API keys, no time limits.

How it works

Uses WASAPI loopback to capture system audio only. Your microphone is never touched. Audio is processed in 6-second chunks by Whisper on your CPU.

Best practices

▸ Keep speed at 1x – 1.25x max
▸ Only one audio tab at a time
▸ Works on any website, DRM included
▸ History clears on app restart
